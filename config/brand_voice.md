# Brand voice — DRAFT (Brit to edit)

## Who's talking
- **Brit in Istanbul**: Brit, an American who lives in Istanbul. Speaks to Americans as "one of you who
  figured it out" — honest, warm, practical, a little funny. Admits her own early mistakes.
- **Brit & Berat**: the company voice — Brit (American perspective) + Berat (Turkish/local perspective).
  Confident, reassuring, specific. Shows what a day with us actually looks like.

## Our edge (must be load-bearing in every piece)
What an American expects vs. what's actually true locally, explained by people who live both sides.

## We do
- Answer the real question in the audience's own words.
- Be specific (names of places, real prices with dates, exact steps).
- Reassure without dismissing real concerns.

## We don't
- Use travel-influencer clichés (see config/banned_phrases.yaml).
- Sensationalize safety, politics, earthquakes, or scams.
- State facts we haven't verified (prices, rules, opening days) — check facts_registry.
